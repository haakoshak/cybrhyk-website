How it works

The six maturity levels

  1. 01

    Level 0

    Non-existent: the area is not addressed.

  2. 02

    Level 1

    Ad-hoc: controls depend on individuals.

  3. 03

    Level 2

    Repeatable but informal: practices repeat but are not defined.

  4. 04

    Level 3

    Structured and formalized: the minimum SAMA requires.

  5. 05

    Level 4

    Managed and measurable: controls measured with KPIs.

  6. 06

    Level 5

    Adaptive: continuous improvement driven by intelligence.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the SAMA Cyber Security Framework?

It is the Saudi Central Bank's framework for cybersecurity in the financial sector. It is organised into 4 domains and 32 subdomains, each assessed on a maturity scale from 0 to 5.

What maturity level do we need?

At least Level 3, Structured and formalized, which means policies and controls are defined, approved and implemented.

Who does the framework apply to?

Institutions regulated by the Saudi Central Bank, such as banks, insurance companies and payments firms.

Can an outsourced SOC meet SAMA requirements?

Yes, provided the outsourcing itself meets SAMA's requirements for material outsourcing. We design the service and its reporting so you can show how the outsourced work is governed.
