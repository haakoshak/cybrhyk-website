FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is ECC-2:2024?

ECC-2:2024 is the second edition of the NCA's Essential Cybersecurity Controls. It replaced ECC-1:2018 and sets 108 main controls across 4 domains and 28 subdomains.

Who has to comply with ECC-2?

Government entities inside and outside the Kingdom and their affiliates, and private-sector organisations that own, operate or host critical national infrastructure. Non-CNI private companies are covered separately by NCNICC-1:2025.

What does control 1-2-2 require?

That cybersecurity positions are filled by qualified, full-time Saudi professionals. It is one of the controls organisations most often need to plan for.

How long does it take to close ECC-2 gaps?

It depends on your starting point. The gap assessment comes first and gives you a scored baseline and a sequenced roadmap, so the effort is clear before you commit to the remediation work.
