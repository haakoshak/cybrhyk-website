FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a managed SOC?

A managed security operations centre is an outsourced team that monitors your systems around the clock, investigates alerts and responds to incidents. It gives you a 24/7 capability without building and staffing your own centre, which takes around 4.8 people for every seat watched continuously.

How is MDR different from a managed SOC?

Managed detection and response focuses on finding and stopping active threats: detection engineering, threat hunting and hands-on containment. Cyber HAYKALAH delivers both as one service, so the work continues past the alert into the response.

Does our data leave Saudi Arabia?

No. Monitoring runs from an operations centre in the Kingdom, and telemetry is processed and stored in Saudi Arabia in line with NDMO policy.

Does a managed SOC help with ECC-2 compliance?

Yes. Monitoring, logging and incident handling are core ECC-2:2024 requirements, and you receive monthly evidence you can hand to your assessor.
