FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an incident response retainer?

An agreement made before an incident that sets out who you call, how fast the team responds and how the work is run. It removes the delay of finding and contracting a provider in the middle of a crisis.

What should we do first if we suspect a breach?

Isolate affected systems if you can do so safely, preserve logs and evidence, and call your incident response provider. Avoid wiping or rebuilding machines before the evidence is captured.

Do you notify the regulator for us?

We prepare the incident report and evidence your organisation needs for its regulatory notifications. The notification itself is made by your organisation.

Is forensic evidence kept in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Evidence is handled and stored in the Kingdom, with chain-of-custody records.
